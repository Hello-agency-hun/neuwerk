Placeholder How to update this page

10 August 2026

This site has no content management system.

Everything is plain files, which means you can update it with a text editor and an upload. Here is how.

Adding a news article

It takes two steps.

First, open data/news.js. You will see a list of entries. Copy one entry, paste it at the top of the list, and change the fields. The slug becomes the file name, so use only lowercase letters and hyphens. Remove placeholder: true once the article is real. that line is what shows the orange badge.

The image field is the picture that appears on the card in the Media list and as the header image at the top of the article. It is one setting, not two, so the two can never drift apart. Put your file in assets/img/news/ and name it after the slug, then write the path exactly as the other entries do: image: "assets/img/news/my-story.jpg". Leave the field out entirely and the card simply shows no picture. nothing breaks.

Second, go to the media/ folder, duplicate any existing article file, and rename it to match your slug. For a slug of my-story, the file must be media/my-story.html. Open it and replace the heading, the date and the body text. Near the top you will find two lines that point at the header image. Change the file name in both to match the one you set in data/news.js.

Removing an article

Delete its entry from data/news.js and delete the matching file from media/. Do both. leaving the file behind is harmless, but leaving the entry behind creates a broken link.

Adding a job opening

Open data/jobs.js and copy an entry the same way. Jobs have no separate page, so this is a single step. If you have an application link, put it in the url field; leave it empty and the title simply will not be a link.

Updating the legal documents

The documents on the Acting Responsibly page are PDFs in assets/docs/. To publish a real version, replace the PDF file keeping the same file name. Nothing in the HTML needs to change and no link will break.

Images

News pictures go in assets/img/news/, everything else in assets/img/. Save them 1200 to 1600 pixels wide and compress them before upload. a news picture should be well under 300 KB. Large images are the fastest way to make a page feel slow.

Crop news pictures to 16:9, roughly 1200 × 675. The card crops to that ratio anyway, and the article header crops from the centre, so anything important near an edge can be cut off. Avoid pictures with text burnt into them. the header lays a dark wash over the image, and burnt-in text is unreadable through it.

Checking your work

Open index.html in a browser and click through. You do not need a server. If a link does not work, the most likely cause is a slug that does not match its file name.

What you should not edit

Leave the css/ and js/ folders alone, and do not edit the parts of a page between the @partial:header and @partial:footer markers. those are repeated on every page and must stay identical. If the navigation or footer needs to change, that is a developer task.
